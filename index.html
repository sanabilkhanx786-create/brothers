01 — THE BOND

02 — FOR YOU BOTH

"Har baat kehna zaroori nahi hota."

Kabhi ek dusre se arguments honge, kabhi opinions alag honge, kabhi baat bhi nahi hogi.

Lekin asli brotherhood wahi hai jahan differences relationship se bade nahi hote.
